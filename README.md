# moop
matter out of place: a small drone that can pick up plastic trash

## why

India has 6.7 million km of roads, Indians feel free chucking plastic trash out of their car windows, inadequate governance means it doesn't get cleaned up. Result: beautiful natural environments are choked with plastic.

## what

This project contains designs and code for a small (palm-sized), low-cost (under $25 per unit) autonomous drone with a harpoon/gripper mechanism that can identify a piece of trash (possibly under foliage, up or down a hill, behind a fence, or otherwise hard to access from the ground), approach to within striking distance, fire a harpoon/gripper at it to collect it, and then return the offending piece matter to a home-base receptacle (which can be static or mobile).

## how

We're running the project on a volunteer basis. Anyone can contribute their ideas, knowledge, code, engineering skills, or connections. By the same token, anyone can use any of the intellectual property so generated to adapt the design to their own needs, create their own designs, or scale up to create their own businesses.

## who

A small band of plucky idiots who care about this problem and want to build something super cool. Possibly including you?
